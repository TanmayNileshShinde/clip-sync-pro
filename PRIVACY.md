## 🛡️ Zero-Knowledge & No-Logging Guarantee

ClipSync PRO exists to eliminate surveillance, tracking, and cloud data hoarding when moving notes, files, audio, and documents between devices.

- **No Account Required:** You do not need to provide an email, username, phone number, or login to use ClipSync PRO.
- **Client-Side Encryption:** Every note, snippet, and file is encrypted in your browser before transmission. We cannot read, decrypt, or monetize your content under any circumstances.
- **Direct P2P Data Transfers:** File transfers between paired devices stream directly device-to-device over WebRTC. Zero bytes of file data pass through our servers.
- **Ephemeral Storage & Self-Destruction:** Any temporary cloud payload is permanently deleted immediately after it is decrypted (burn-on-read) or after its expiration timer expires.
- **No Analytics & No Third-Party Trackers:** No tracking pixels, Google Analytics, or third-party marketing cookies are present.

---

## 📱 Device Permissions Usage

- **Camera:** Exclusively used on-device via the browser `BarcodeDetector` API to scan QR pairing codes. No camera frames are recorded, saved, or uploaded.
- **Microphone:** Exclusively used locally to record voice memos via the native `MediaRecorder` API upon user action. Audio is encrypted in browser RAM before transmission.
